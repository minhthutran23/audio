## Notes for production
- 11 scenes total: Scene 1 = 6s title card (5s content + 1s transition). Scenes 2-11 = 10s each, with the final ~1s of each reserved as a transition/breathing beat into the next cut — so dialogue in each scene should land around 7-8s of actual speaking, not fill the full 10s.
- Voice pacing: faster, more energetic delivery than a typical narrator read — should feel like snappy back-and-forth, not a slow storybook read.
- Biscuit does not speak English lines. His "thoughts" are now voiced by the Narrator describing his intent/reaction (e.g., "Biscuit's already decided it's his now"), while Biscuit himself only barks for punctuation/comic timing.
- Dialogue tone: Mel = warm, a little dramatic, never actually angry at Biscuit. Narrator = playful, deadpan-amused, the one doing most of the "translating" of what Biscuit is thinking/planning.
- Scene 10 (Learning Moment) is still an overlay graphic during the tail end of Scene 9 / start of Scene 11, not a separate filmed clip — let me know if you'd rather make it its own full clip instead.
